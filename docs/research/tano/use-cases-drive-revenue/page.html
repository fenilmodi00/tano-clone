How it works.

Your customers’ favourite creators, found, set up with a code and a link, and coached on what sells. Every order lands in your own reporting.

01Run by Tano

Sourcing

Tano finds creators whose audiences are new to you and already buy through links, working from your creator personas and within your guardrails. Each is scored on how likely they are to bring new customers, and only those above the threshold go forward.

02Run by Tano

Onboarding

Every creator gets a code and a tracking link, connected to your Shopify store and placed in the commission group that suits them, such as 15% of every attributed order. Each sale they drive is attributed to them, and a customer’s first order is marked as new.

03Run by Tano

Incentivisation

Tano’s agents coach every creator one to one, from how to use the link sticker to what to post next, working from your incentives and the sales in your affiliate reporting. Bonuses and higher commission go to what you want more of, such as new customers, so effort follows results.

04Run by Tano

Tracking and measurement

Revenue, new customers, CAC and lifetime value update live from your own affiliate reporting, measured against the objective you set. You see which creators and incentives are paying back, and Tano shifts the programme towards them.

Incentivise, coach, reward. Then do itagain, and again.

Every programme teaches us what to improve: which creators reach the right people, what motivates them to post, and which activity leads to first-time orders. Those signals shape the next round.

  1. 01

    We learn what the ideal creator profile looks like

    We start from your customers, their interests, their routines, and then find compatible creators for your identity. From there we build your Creator Personas: matching audience fit, content quality, and brand-safety criteria that every later recruit is measured against.

  2. 02

    We recruit, gift, and get creators posting

    Tano finds creators who fit your personas, then manages outreach, onboarding, product gifting and guidance. We track each creator from first contact through posting and sales, so your team can see what’s happening across the programme.

  3. 03

    We reward what sells, and recruit more of it

    Creators earn commission on sales, with agreed milestones unlocking further rewards. We compare first-time orders and acquisition cost across Creator Personas: where a persona beats your CAC target, our agents recruit more of it; and where it doesn’t, the budget moves to match.

  4. 04

    We keep testing what leads to purchases

    Our agents test outreach, products, content formats and incentives within your guidelines. Each creator’s response helps shape their follow-up, while creator sentiment shows us which rewards and support encourage people to keep posting.
