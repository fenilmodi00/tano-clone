How Bloom & Wild made creators a revenue channel

Creator partnerships drive monthly revenue across brands and markets.

Bloom & Wild’s seasonal products create a constant need to reach customers with fresh, relevant creative. Its brand team worked with creators through monthly gifting, while performance marketers wanted creator partnerships that could drive orders. The opportunity was to connect those activities into one acquisition programme.

The Problem

The team wanted creator partnerships to drive orders, but slow onboarding, overlapping relationships and manual administration limited growth.

Growing the affiliate programme

Traditional affiliate onboarding was slow. The team wanted to bring more creators into performance activity without adding more manual work.

Bringing brand and performance together

Brand and performance teams approached creators from different angles. They needed to collaborate without competing for the same relationships.

Removing work that limited growth

Outreach, fee negotiations, contracts, unique codes and commission calculations were manual. Seasonal products also required fresh creative every month.

The Solution

Tano connected creator sourcing, content capture and affiliate activity around Bloom & Wild’s existing teams. Automation removed repetitive work without making creator relationships feel impersonal.

  1. 01

    Creator affiliate onboarding

    Tano gave the team a route to onboard creators for performance activity and grow the affiliate programme.

  2. 02

    A continuing supply of creators

    Fresh creators supported monthly gifting and seasonal content needs, giving brand and performance teams more room to collaborate.

  3. 03

    Choose content before buying rights

    The team could review what creators produced, identify suitable content and request advertising rights, instead of committing upfront to unseen creative.

  4. 04

    Captured content for paid media

    Captured posts created a content library the team could return to when planning paid-media campaigns, rather than relying on screenshots and manual filing.

  5. 05

    Reporting built around the business

    Campaign structures and reporting reflected Bloom & Wild’s brands, markets, teams and terminology.

  6. 06

    Less administration, familiar workflow

    Less repetitive administration freed up the team’s time. Slack kept communication within its existing workflow, supported by responsive people who understood the account.

The Results

Bloom & Wild has established creators as a revenue-driving acquisition channel with Tano, generating revenue every month and achieving 125% higher click-through rates and 10% lower cost per acquisition.

The programme has expanded across brands and markets, bringing brand and performance teams together to drive acquisition through creator partnerships. Less time spent sourcing creators, managing communications and coordinating product sends means more capacity to grow the channel.

Tano enables the team to operate at a scale that would otherwise require at least one additional hire.
