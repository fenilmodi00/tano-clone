How Skin+Me unlocked acquisition with overlooked creators

Tano finds creators beyond Skin+Me’s usual profile, giving Katie more time to build ambassador relationships.

Skin+Me provides personalised skincare on subscription. Its ambassador programme connects the business with creators who introduce customers to its products. Growing that channel means finding more suitable partners while maintaining the personal relationships that help ambassadors succeed.

The Problem

Skin+Me wanted creator partnerships to drive customer acquisition, but manual work limited both discovery and the time available to develop promising relationships.

Looking beyond the usual creator profile

Potential performers were easy to pass over when the team had limited time to assess creators outside its usual profile of partners.

Manual work competing with growth

Sourcing profiles was only the start. Outreach, follow-ups, programme questions, tracking links and codes all needed attention before a creator could become an active ambassador.

Too little time for relationships

Katie was balancing discovery and onboarding with managing the programme. Repetitive administration left less time to engage ambassadors, recognise strong performers and develop new ideas.

The Solution

Tano combines broader creator discovery with managed sourcing, outreach and onboarding. Katie keeps the judgement and personal relationships that turn promising creators into productive ambassadors.

  1. 01

    Discover overlooked creators

    Tano brings creators beyond the usual profile into consideration. Katie can assess promising candidates without first investing her time in every sourcing and outreach conversation.

  2. 02

    Turn interest into active partnerships

    Tano handles sourcing, outreach, follow-ups and routine programme questions, with support for links, codes and onboarding. The focus is getting suitable creators into the acquisition programme.

  3. 03

    Give relationships dedicated attention

    Katie can focus on VIP relationships, personalised gifts, handwritten notes and direct engagement—making ambassadors feel valued and giving them reasons to stay involved.

  4. 04

    Combine AI scale with human judgement

    Katie was initially sceptical about AI in a relationship-led channel. Working with Tano changed her mind: automation supports her judgement and personal touch rather than replacing them.

The Results

Skin+Me has unlocked a source of customer acquisition through creators the team might otherwise have overlooked. In a single month, four of Skin+Me’s five top-performing creators were discovered and onboarded by Tano.
